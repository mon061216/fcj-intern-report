Participating in the **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program has been a highly meaningful technical learning journey during my university years. Below is my honest evaluation and constructive feedback aimed at helping the organizing team and the FCAJ community continue to enhance future cohorts.

---

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment & Self-Study Culture**  
The AWS Vietnam office at Bitexco Financial Tower is modern, inspiring, and professional. However, the program heavily emphasizes **independent self-study and self-research**. Given the vast scope of Cloud Computing and Generative AI, along with the intricacy of AWS services, spending long hours navigating complex technical challenges independently can sometimes become overwhelming and discouraging, especially when hitting hard bugs without immediate guidance.  
*Expectation:* If the program could incorporate short periodic sync-ups (such as weekly check-ins or office hours) where student groups can share roadblocks and receive quick pointers from seniors, the learning experience would feel much more encouraging and sustainable.

**2. Support from Mentors and Program Admins**  
I am deeply grateful for the dedication of the mentors. Mentors provide enthusiastic support, detailed explanations, and encourage interns to understand root causes rather than simply spoon-feeding answers. Their advice regarding architecture optimization, IAM security, and local-first development significantly accelerated my engineering maturity. The admin team was also consistently welcoming and supportive whenever interns visited the office.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The curriculum aligns closely with and strongly reinforces my **Software Engineering** major at FPT University HCMC. Hands-on experience in architecting Serverless systems, writing Lambda functions, managing SQS queues, querying DynamoDB NoSQL tables, and integrating GenAI via APIs allowed me to bridge academic concepts with real-world enterprise engineering.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
FCAJ provides an outstanding practical environment backed by extensive AWS resources. Beyond distributed systems design and coding, I cultivated the discipline of reading official technical documentation in English, publishing technical blog posts on the AWS Study Group community, authoring detailed multi-part workshop guides, and analyzing cloud economics.

**5. Internship Location**  
The venue for meetups and events at Bitexco Financial Tower (District 1) is prestigious and well-equipped. However, this downtown location is relatively distant from where most technology students reside and study (such as FPT University and universities located in Thu Duc City / District 9 / the University Village area). Long commutes during rush hours consume substantial time and travel expenses for students.  
*Suggestion:* The organizing committee might consider partnering with co-working spaces or establishing flexible hub locations closer to major university clusters to make in-person attendance more convenient.

**6. Communication Channels & Internal Announcements**  
Internal communications could be made more consistent and structured. Important logistical details—especially **office dress codes**, building security protocols, and specific event schedules—should be communicated earlier and more clearly.  
*Suggestion:* Official announcements should preferably be sent via **Email** or clearly **pinned** in the main community communication channels so that students do not miss essential updates amidst ongoing group chats.

---

### Key Reflections

* **What did you find most satisfying during the internship?**  
  The enthusiastic guidance from mentors and the rewarding feeling of successfully building an end-to-end cloud solution ("Ask First - ScamProtection") leveraging modern Serverless and Multimodal AI technologies.

* **What do you think should be improved for future cohorts?**  
  Achieving a healthier balance between independent research and guided group discussions to reduce intern fatigue; standardizing communication channels for logistical updates (such as dress codes and schedules via email and pinned messages); and considering more student-accessible locations.

* **Would you recommend this program to your peers?**  
  **Yes, absolutely.** It offers exceptional hands-on practical value and serves as a strong launchpad for any software engineering student pursuing a career in Cloud Computing and modern AI engineering.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* Host short weekly technical syncs (30–60 minutes) where interns can present progress and unblock technical bottlenecks.
* Provide an onboarding handbook covering office dress codes, building guidelines, and template resources during the orientation week.
* I look forward to staying actively engaged with the AWS Study Group community and participating in future AWS student initiatives!
